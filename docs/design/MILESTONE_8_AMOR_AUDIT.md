# Milestone 8 AMOR Design Audit

## Status

Milestone 8 adds a more analytical site surface while retaining AMOR restraint: petroleum and teal as primary structure, sand/paper backgrounds, amber for stress and rewired edges, dense but readable research panels, and visible warnings about model status.

## Additions Checked

- Production-network explorer uses graph-analysis controls without turning the site into a marketing page.
- Labor-market explorer is a functional mechanism view, not decorative illustration.
- Manuscript panel keeps research status, evidence, limitations, and reproducibility close to the simulations.
- Mobile layout collapses graph, labor, and manuscript panels into one-column flows.
- No new external assets were added.
