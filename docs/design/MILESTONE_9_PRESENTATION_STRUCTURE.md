# Milestone 9 Presentation Structure

Milestone 9 reorganizes the web app from a long vertical listing into a research workspace.

## Views

- Overview: the research brief, current endpoint, and static artifact status.
- Simulator: scenario controls, metadata, treatment-minus-baseline charts, and asset channels.
- Networks: graph-analysis production-network explorer and sector stress summary.
- Labor: employer-worker transmission explorer and labor metrics.
- Manuscript: manuscript status, literature anchors, limitations, and reproducibility.

## Design Rationale

The previous Milestone 8 page exposed the right capabilities but listed too many surfaces at once. The new structure keeps the computational laboratory feel while reducing cognitive load for non-programmer readers.
