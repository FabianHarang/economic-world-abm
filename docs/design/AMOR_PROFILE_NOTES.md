## Identity Summary

AMOR is the Center for Applied Mathematics and Operations Research. The design profile describes the identity as precise, rigorous, modern, and purposeful. The mark is a connected geometric system with a single warm amber node representing the optimum, solution, or insight emerging from complexity.

## Primary Visual Elements

- Primary mark: Geodesic mark.
- Secondary marks: Network, Descent/Contour, and Polytope.
- Wordmark: AMOR in Space Grotesk, uppercase, generous tracking.
- Core color family: petroleum and teal.
- Warm accent: sand/amber, used sparingly for the key node, highlighted series, or important status.
- Neutrals: warm paper, paper-pure, mist, line, ink.

## Logo and Mark Use

- Preserve aspect ratio.
- Keep clear space at least equal to the symbol radius.
- Use the horizontal lockup by default.
- Use stacked lockup where width is tight.
- Use icon alone only where AMOR is already established.
- Reversed mark may be used on petroleum backgrounds.
- Do not rotate, skew, stretch, recolor off-palette, or swap the wordmark font.

## Project-Specific Application

For the economic-world ABM, the design must support scientific caution. AMOR identity should provide coherence and trust, but uncertainty, model limitations, and assumptions must remain visible.

Charts should use:

- petroleum for baseline or primary reference;
- teal for treatment or main comparison;
- amber for highlighted effect, optimum, or warning point;
- glacier and clay for secondary series;
- line colors from the AMOR neutral scale;
- visible legends and direct labels where possible.
